# What's new in Lichen

## Messages and radio
- Notifications: direct messages, channel messages, mentions and failed deliveries can now notify you, one switch per kind.
- MeshCore got a home grid of tiles, contact details with trace and discover path, channel details, share strings and contact export and import.
- You can choose how often the badge announces itself, whether it is a flood or a zero-hop advert, and whether it adds new contacts by itself.
- Wi-Fi and LoRa have their own switches, plus an airplane mode. Switched-off radios show dim, not red.

## Home and screens
- A Clock app on the home screen: the Swiss railway style station clock full size, with the date and where the time came from.
- Tiles show a red count when something is new (unread messages, new crashes).
- A clock screensaver (a Swiss railway style station clock with a sweeping second hand, or a digital clock), and a pocket lock that keeps the keys from doing anything in your bag.
- The status bar shows the Wi-Fi strength, a centred clock and small arrows when the radio sends or receives.
- The colours on the screen are now correct (red and blue were swapped).
- The shape keys are fixed shortcuts that work everywhere.

## Looking after the badge
- A Power app: restart or power off cleanly.
- Notification centre, toasts, a status LED for unread notices.
- The badge watches its own temperature and switches the radios off before it gets too warm.
- Factory reset from Settings, the console, or a key combination at start-up. It also wipes old BadgeVMS leftovers, Wi-Fi password included.
- Crash log app: see what went wrong, and delete entries.
- Updates over Wi-Fi can switch themselves off again afterwards.

## Behind the scenes
- Safer saving of your MeshCore identity: it is never replaced when it cannot be read.
- Less internal memory used, and quieter, better sorted log messages.
