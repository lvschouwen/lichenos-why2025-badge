# What's new in Lichen

## Settings
- Sliders always show their track, also at the lowest value.
- About > For developers: Last write error reads "none" when nothing failed, and the C6 registry caps show in hex.

## MeshCore
- About and the radio pages show the radio the badge really uses. A note says so when it differs from what MeshCore asks for.
- `fw mesh` prints the radio in use and how old that reading is.

## Under the hood
- Each app gets its own identity from the system, the first step to apps that can't reach each other's data.
- The radio chip's firmware has a fixed version, so an update only reflashes it when it really changed.
