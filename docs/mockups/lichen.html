A lichen isn't one organism but two: a fungus and an alga living as one. The alga makes food from light, the fungus gives it shelter and holds on to the rock. Neither could live there alone.

This badge works the same way. The P4 does the thinking and the screen; the C6 talks to the world over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and LoRa. And like lichen, it spreads slowly from stone to stone: one node, then a mesh.
